What works in this build

  • Working: accounts and sessions, admin approval, pin-based reporting with duplicate warnings, one confirmation per person per incident, the incident lifecycle, filters, road routing (OSRM public service) with hazard checks, and live weather (Open-Meteo, no key).
  • Simulated: AI-detected incidents are demo seeds until the NLP pipeline is connected. Duplicate matching uses location and category only.
  • Needs a backend: enforced roles, server-side credits, a shared feed between users, and secure photo storage. Browser-only checks can be bypassed by anyone with developer tools.
  • Needs your own key: the TomTom traffic and OpenWeather rain overlays. Keys entered on the map are kept only for the current tab.
